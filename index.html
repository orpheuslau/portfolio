01 // Background & Philosophy

Bridging computing rigor with human welfare.

Two decades spent deploying frontline digital transformations for 4,000+ staff and diverse community cohorts.

I am a technology leader with over twenty years of deep, dedicated experience across the social services sector. My career is defined by a unique dual foundation: holding formal degrees in both Computing Science (1st Class Honours) and Social Work and Social Sciences (mgt) from universities in Hong Kong and UK. Over the years, I have architected and deployed enterprise case information systems, biometric credential managers, augmented reality platforms, and high-throughput computer vision workstations.

Rather than pursuing innovation in a vacuum, I specialize in translating high-stakes human challenges into production-grade AI solutions. Currently pursuing a Master in Artificial Intelligence at the University of Leeds, my technical focus centers on (a privacy-preserving dual-LLM candidate screening engine with zero PII leakage), (a multi-agent system decoupling exact JUPAS admission score math from empathetic Cantonese counseling), the (an enterprise hybrid RAG engine delivering clause-grounded statutory guidance across complex welfare funding manuals), and the (an archival RAG interactive docent serving eight decades of heritage with multilingual kiosk narration and strict curatorial guardrails).
